《观测站的一天》
A Day at the Observatory

第一章 清晨的记录
Chapter 1: Morning Notes

林溪推开青河观测站的窗，看见云层正缓缓移向山谷。
Lin Xi opened a window at Qinghe Observatory and saw the clouds slowly drifting toward the valley.

“晨光计划的第一份记录，就从今天开始。”她说。
“The first record for the Morninglight Project starts today,” she said.

桌上放着三本笔记，每一本都标明了日期。
Three notebooks lay on the desk, each marked with a date.

……
…

她没有急着下结论，而是把看到的变化逐项写了下来。
She did not rush to a conclusion. Instead, she wrote down each change she had observed.

第二章 返回阅览室
Chapter 2: Back to the Reading Room

午后，林溪带着记录回到阅览室。
In the afternoon, Lin Xi returned to the reading room with her notes.

她把今天的笔记与昨天的放在一起，对照相同时间的云量。
She laid today's notes beside yesterday's and compared the cloud cover at the same times.

“有些变化需要多看几天，才知道是不是规律。”
“Some changes need a few more days of observation before we can tell whether they follow a pattern.”

窗外又起了风，桌上的书页轻轻翻动。
The wind picked up outside, gently turning the pages of a book on the desk.
